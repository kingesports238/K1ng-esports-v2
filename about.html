OUR STORY

ONE NAME. ONE CROWN.

K1NG Esports was created with one simple idea: build an organisation where competitive players can chase their ambitions and represent something bigger than themselves.

From competitive teams to creators and future academy talent, the goal is to create a community where hard work, discipline and confidence matter.

The crown represents the standard we set for ourselves. We don't just want to participate. We want to compete.
